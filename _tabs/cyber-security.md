Cyber security looks different when you're the person responsible for keeping a school, small business or community organisation working. There may be no dedicated security team. The person reviewing a suspicious email might also be fixing the Wi-Fi, preparing a budget and explaining to the board why an old server needs replacing.

I'm Nicholas O'Sullivan, usually Nick, an IT and cyber security professional based in Townsville. This is where I bring together my regional research, incident reporting and practical writing about the systems people depend on. My starting point is fairly simple: understand what matters, know who is responsible, and check that the controls actually work.

## North Queensland Cyber Watch

I started Cyber Watch to turn regional incidents into useful questions for other organisations. What is confirmed? What is still alleged? Which dependency or decision deserves a closer look in our own environment?

The [Regional Queensland Cyber Security Incident Tracker](/cyber-security/incident-tracker/) brings the entries together with dates, locations, source links and evidence labels. It distinguishes incidents at regional organisations from national platform incidents that may affect people here. It is a record of the reporting collected on this site, not a census of every attack.

You can also [read the full Cyber Watch series](/tags/north-queensland-cyber-watch/).

## Practical resources for organisations

- [Practical cyber security for small organisations](/posts/practical-cyber-security-for-small-organisations/) starts with changes a small team can understand and maintain.
- [The CIA Triad](/posts/cia-triad-foundations-of-cyber-security/) explains confidentiality, integrity and availability, including why an outage matters even when no data is reported stolen.
- [Identity and access management](/posts/asset-security-identity-access-management/) looks at who can reach your information and systems.
- [Beyond the Essential Eight: cybersecurity governance for Australian schools](/posts/beyond-essential-eight-school-cybersecurity-governance/) connects technical controls to purpose, ownership and investment decisions.
- [School cyber audits and managed security](/posts/school-cyber-audits-vs-managed-security-part-one/) examines what different forms of assurance actually tell a school.
- [Can They Email As Me?](/posts/can-they-email-as-me/) explains the email authentication tool I built and the limits of what a public check can establish.

For an active incident, start with [ASD's reporting and assistance channels](https://www.cyber.gov.au/submit-a-report). For personal identity and cyber support, [IDCARE](https://www.idcare.org/) provides assistance. An article or a website check cannot investigate your own environment.
