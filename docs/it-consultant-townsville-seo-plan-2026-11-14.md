## 2. Diagnosis from the current site

Direct live checks on 14 November found:

- `/consulting/` returns HTTP 200, has a self-referencing HTTPS/www canonical, and has no observed meta or HTTP `noindex` directive.
- Its rendered title is **Cyber Security Consultant Townsville & North Queensland | Nicholas O'Sullivan**. Its H1 is **Cyber Security & IT Consulting in Townsville**.
- The homepage identifies Nick as an IT and cyber security **professional**; its main service link says **Consulting and contact**. Navigation says **Consult / Contact**.
- The consulting page already offers Microsoft 365 reviews, network advice and technology planning, with a booking link and contact section. About, the cyber hub and several articles already link to it.
- `robots.txt` permits this page and references the sitemap. The sitemap includes consulting URLs. HTTP and non-www consulting requests each permanently redirect to the correct address.

**Inference:** the current positioning gives cyber expertise and professional identity more emphasis than general IT consulting. Clearer commercial intent, stronger proof and relevant links are the best first experiment. There is no obvious basic crawling fault in these checks. They do not establish Google's selected canonical, current index status, Core Web Vitals or backlink strength.

Comparable providers illustrate the search intent: [D-stress](https://www.dstress.tech/it-consulting) has a dedicated Townsville IT consulting page, while [Curity](https://www.curity.com.au/services/it-consulting/) explains decisions it helps with, engagement steps and second opinions. These are content comparisons, not a verified Townsville Google ranking survey.

## 3. First two weeks: improve the existing destination

**A. Record a fresh baseline before release.** In Search Console, use Web, Australia, the `www` host and the latest complete 28 days versus the preceding 28 days. Repeat for mobile and desktop where volume supports it. Filter each consulting query and export its Pages tab. Inspect `/consulting/` and the UniFi page for index status and Google's selected canonical. Keep an unfiltered view alongside Australia so the new baseline is not compared directly with the differently scoped supplied totals.

**B. Keep `/consulting/` and revise its positioning.** No new exact-match URL or redirect is needed for this experiment. Proposed copy:

- Page title: **IT Consultant Townsville | Nick O'Sullivan**. Check the generated HTML so the theme does not append a second name.
- H1: **IT consulting in Townsville**.
- Description: **Townsville IT consultant Nick O'Sullivan helps businesses, schools and charities with Microsoft 365, networks, cyber security and technology planning.**
- Opening: **I'm Nick O'Sullivan, a Townsville-based IT consultant helping businesses, schools and community organisations make practical technology decisions. I advise on Microsoft 365, networks, cyber security and technology planning, including second opinions on proposed upgrades.**

Retain substantive cyber services and links. Add clear sections explaining:

1. When to engage Nick: an upgrade quote to assess, unreliable Wi-Fi, unclear cloud ownership, an upcoming project or a technology budget to plan.
2. What the engagement produces: agreed scope, findings, prioritised recommendations, design or documentation where included, and a handover discussion. Promise only deliverables actually offered.
3. How it works: initial discussion, agreed scope and fee, review or project, documented next steps. Retain accurate availability and separation from school employment.
4. Why Nick: a short relevant experience summary, existing ACS and Ubiquiti credentials, and two links to evidence of work.
5. Buyer questions: working with an existing provider, on-site versus remote work, fees and quoting, one-off projects, and who delivers implementation.

Retain the booking and email routes; use **Discuss your IT project** as the primary action. Clear, descriptive titles and visible page wording follow [Google's title guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/title-link) and [Search Essentials](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials). This is an experiment, not a guaranteed ranking lift.

**C. Give the page stronger support.** Update the homepage lead to naturally identify Nick as a Townsville IT consultant while retaining cyber expertise. Change the homepage service button to **IT consulting in Townsville** and the navigation label to **IT Consulting**. Keep contact easy to find.

Add contextual links from three to five genuinely relevant existing pieces: the network-upgrade archive, network modernisation articles, school technology/governance writing and the existing consultancy article where useful. Existing About and cyber-hub links can remain. UniFi articles should continue to link directly to the specialist UniFi page. Use varied, descriptive wording; do not add commercial links mechanically to every local news article. See [Google's link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).

## 4. Weeks three to six: add evidence and local credibility

**Publish two useful examples of work.** Reuse material already available where possible. One could explain a network design or upgrade decision; another could cover a Microsoft 365 or technology planning review, if a suitable documented engagement exists. Show the starting problem, constraints, Nick's actual role, recommendations and evidenced outcome. Label an archived proposal or educational exercise accurately; do not turn it into a completed client success story. Obtain consent for identifiable client material and testimonials.

**Write one buyer-focused guide.** Suggested topic: **When should a Townsville business use an IT consultant?** Explain consultant versus ongoing support, what to prepare, common deliverables and how scope affects fees. Link to `/consulting/`. Prioritise this and practical examples over increasing general publishing volume.

**Clarify the relationship between websites.** On this site, Nick is the individual consultant and `/consulting/` is the general advisory destination. Suburban Secure explains its specialist security services; Townsville Computer Man handles its repair/support offering. Keep the real ownership relationship explicit and use relevant links between distinct services. Multiple sites are not evidence of a penalty, but duplicating the same local consulting page across them would complicate measurement and positioning.

**Review the existing Google Business Profile, if eligible.** First identify which actual business it represents and whether the service meets in-person eligibility. Review the real-world name, available accurate consulting category, services, contact details, service area and appropriate website destination. Link to this site's consulting page only if it correctly represents that business. Do not create another profile merely for the personal domain. Invite honest reviews after completed work and respond to them; do not script keyword-rich reviews. Google describes local ranking in terms of relevance, distance and prominence, with [profile guidance](https://support.google.com/business/answer/7091?hl=en-en) and [eligibility/representation rules](https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177?hl=en).

**Strengthen real references.** Update existing professional profiles and legitimate partner/speaker biographies to describe IT consulting in Townsville where accurate. Seek a handful of relevant references from actual collaborators, events or organisations. No paid link packages or mass directory submissions are required. Any outreach remains a future owner action, not part of this planning work.

## 5. Weeks seven to twelve: evaluate and adjust

Track these separately every 28 days:

| Measure | Baseline or action | Success signal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Four exact local consulting variants | Supplied baseline: 79 impressions / 2 clicks across three months; refresh with matching filters | More visibility and clicks in comparable windows |
| `it consulting townsville` position | Supplied average 14.96 | Working goal of sustained average top-ten visibility; not a forecast |
| URL shown for consulting queries | Unknown until query-to-page export | `/consulting/` becomes the clear relevant destination |
| Qualified consulting enquiries | No baseline supplied | Track actual suitable enquiries and booked conversations |
| Cyber and branded queries/pages | Capture matching pre-release baseline | Existing valuable visibility remains stable or improves |
| Maps/Profile interactions | Only if a relevant profile exists | Report separately from organic web performance |

For the initial exact-query group, use the Search Console regex `^it (consultant|consultants|consulting|consultancy) townsville$`. Review newly emerging variants separately so group expansion is not mistaken for like-for-like growth.

Add or verify measurement of booking-link clicks, email reveals and email clicks. These are intent signals, not completed leads. The current local contact script has no explicit analytics events; the GA configuration alone does not verify that these actions or completed bookings are measured. Attribute confirmed enquiries through the booking system or a simple enquiry log, without sending personal message content to analytics.

At approximately day 30, verify crawling/indexing and whether the intended page receives impressions. At day 60, inspect query/page changes and any weak conversion points. At day 90, decide whether to retain the positioning or refine it. Compare equal reporting windows and allow for Christmas/January seasonality; two clicks over three months is too small for precise growth forecasts.

- If Google selects another URL, inspect its intent and internal links before considering consolidation.
- If impressions increase but clicks do not, review the actual snippets and query relevance.
- If relevant visitors arrive but enquiries do not, improve proof, scope, availability and contact flow.
- If visibility remains flat after indexing is verified, invest in stronger project evidence and relevant external references. Add a specialist service page only when the offer and query evidence justify it.
