Short biography

Nicholas “Nick” O’Sullivan is a Townsville-based IT and cyber security professional with more than 17 years of experience across small business, education and community organisations. His work spans infrastructure, cloud platforms, identity, governance and practical technology leadership. He writes North Queensland Cyber Watch and researches regional cyber incidents, training and careers. Nick holds a Graduate Diploma of Information Technology (Networking) with Distinction from Central Queensland University and a Bachelor of Multimedia Journalism from James Cook University. He is an ACS Certified Technologist and provides independent technology and security advice through Suburban Australia and its specialist service, Suburban Secure, in Townsville.

Longer biography

Nicholas O’Sullivan, usually known as Nick, was born and raised in Townsville. His professional background includes operating a local IT support business, working across Queensland state school environments and, since October 2024, working as an IT Manager in an independent school environment.

His experience covers infrastructure planning, Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, identity and endpoint management, networking, service delivery and community-sector governance. He holds a Graduate Diploma of Information Technology (Networking) with Distinction from Central Queensland University, a Bachelor of Multimedia Journalism from James Cook University, and ACS Certified Technologist recognition.

Nick’s cyber security capability has developed through hands-on technology work and continuing professional development. His writing connects that experience with public-source research, distinguishing confirmed facts from allegations and explaining what an incident can reasonably tell another organisation about its own readiness.

His North Queensland Cyber Watch series examines incidents with regional connections. His wider research covers cyber security jobs, training pathways, regional coordination and the responsibilities of schools and small organisations. He also provides consulting through Suburban Australia and Suburban Secure, separately from his school employment. That commercial interest is disclosed in his writing.
