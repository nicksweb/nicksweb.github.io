IT & Cyber Security Consulting in Townsville

Practical technology advice for businesses, schools and community organisations from Townsville and North Queensland.

I’m Nicholas O’Sullivan, usually Nick. I provide IT and cyber security consulting through Suburban Australia and its specialist service, Suburban Secure. I help small businesses, charities, churches and schools understand their technology, decide what needs attention and plan improvements they can maintain.

How I can help

You might need a review of your Microsoft 365 environment, advice before replacing a network, or a clearer plan for a technology project. I bring experience across school IT, small business support, cloud platforms, infrastructure and community organisations.

Cyber security and Microsoft 365 reviews

Review identity and administrator access, email security, cloud settings, endpoint management and backup arrangements. Identify practical improvements and put them in an order your organisation can act on.

Network and infrastructure advice

Assess Wi-Fi, networks, servers, virtualisation and device management. Plan upgrades and improvements around the way your organisation works, the equipment you have and the budget available.

Ubiquiti UniFi network design and quote reviews

Technology planning and project support

Turn a technology problem into a clear scope, priorities and next steps. Get help with systems integration, procurement decisions, vendor coordination, documentation and project delivery.

Working together

Start by telling me about your organisation, the problem you want to solve and any timing or budget constraints. We can discuss whether a review, a defined project or advice on a specific decision would be useful. Scope, availability and fees are agreed before consultancy work begins.

My consultancy work is based in Townsville, with enquiries welcome from across North Queensland. Engagements are scoped around the organisation and undertaken separately from my school employment.
